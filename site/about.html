About CampusConnect

A student-run fest concept that brings every department and club onto one stage.

What is CampusConnect?

CampusConnect is a fictional college fest used as the demo site for this DevOps practical. It shows how a normal website can be packaged and served the same way on any machine.

Why a college fest?

A fest lets students try new skills, meet people outside their class and show their talent. Technical, cultural and sports events all get equal space.

Event categories

  • Technical — hackathon, robotics, coding quiz
  • Cultural — dance, theatre, open mic
  • Sports — football, table tennis
  • Workshops — hands-on learning sessions
  • Competitions — treasure hunt, pitch contest

Clubs and community

Sample clubs that would take part in a fest like this.

Coding Club

Runs hackathons, quizzes and tech talks.

Drama & Dance Society

Organises stage shows and street plays.

Sports Committee

Manages tournaments and referees.

Design Circle

Creates posters and teaches UI basics.
